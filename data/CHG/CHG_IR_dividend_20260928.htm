  3. Dividend Policy and Payment

Subsidiary companies of the Company have dividend payment policy for no less than 50% of net profit from the subsidiary company’s consolidated financial statement after legal reserve and other reserves have been deducted. Dividend payment may change as deemed appropriate depend on operating result, cash flow, business growth, investment plan, financial status, terms and conditions in various contracts that the Company is legally abide, and any future necessities.
