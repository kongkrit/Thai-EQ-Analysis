About Company

Praram 9 Hospital Public Company Limited (hereafter “the hospital” or “the company”) was established in 1992

on a 6-rai land plot on Rama IX Road. The hospital provides a comprehensive range of tertiary care services, including health promotion, preventive healthcare, diagnosis, treatment and rehabilitation. The range covers the medical areas of Obstetrics and Gynecology, Surgery, Medicine, Pediatrics, Orthopedist, as well as medical services for patients with specific needs and complicated diseases provided by Praram 9 Kidney Transplantation Institute, Praram 9 Cardiovascular Institute and a total of 20 specialized medical centers. In addition, Praram 9 Hospital is committed to consistently providing the patients with the best-in-class medical services at reasonable costs according to the Value for Money principle.

  • 204 Registered Beds Current capacity
